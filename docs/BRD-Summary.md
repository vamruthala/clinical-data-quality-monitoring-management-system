# Business Requirements Summary
- **Business question:** How can study teams find incomplete records, QC failures, and submission blockers early?
- **Scope:** 300 participants, four sites, and clinical, EEG, wearable, and biospecimen data.
- **Decision supported:** Prioritize quality issues and review readiness before DCC submission.
- **Success measures:** Completeness/readiness, check counts, issue status, severity, and SLA tracking.
- **Stakeholders:** Specific sponsor and site-owner roles are not named in the source.
