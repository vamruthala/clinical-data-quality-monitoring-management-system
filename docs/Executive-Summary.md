# Executive Summary

- **Need:** Track completeness, QC failures, issues, and DCC readiness across study sites and data streams.
- **Approach:** Participant/metadata tracking, automated checks, issue triage, SLA monitoring, and Tableau.
- **Result:** **5,324** checks, **891** issues, **94%** completeness, and **91%** reported readiness.
- **Value:** Helps focus data-quality follow-up before submission; no clinical outcome claims are made.
